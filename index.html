01

Start writing

On iPhone and iPad, tap a note space to open it. On Mac, click the Seven Square icon in the menu bar, then choose a numbered space. Write in Source and switch to Preview to read Markdown. Changes save automatically.

02

iCloud sync

Sign in to the same iCloud account on your devices. Seven Square uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to sync your notes. Allow time for changes to arrive; on iPhone and iPad, use the cloud button to refresh. If sync is delayed, check your internet connection and iCloud availability. Keep a separate copy of important writing before troubleshooting.

03

Need help?

Email adaebea@gmail.com with your device model, system version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please avoid including private notes, passwords, or other sensitive information.
