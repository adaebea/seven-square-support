Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Your notes.
Your space.

Effective October 21, 2026. This policy applies to Seven Square for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, developed by Hui Zhang.

Notes and preferences

Seven Square stores your notes and app preferences on your device, including a local backup used to protect saved writing. The app does not send your notes to a developer-operated server. We do not have access to the notes stored by the app.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, Seven Square uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to synchronize notes and related editing state across devices signed in to the same iCloud account. This data is handled through Apple’s services and is subject to your iCloud settings and Apple’s privacy policy. Seven Square does not require a separate app account.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your notes for advertising.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and diagnose the reported issue. Please avoid sending sensitive note content. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Your choices

You can edit or clear note text within the app. Because changes may synchronize to your other devices, keep a separate copy before clearing important writing. Removing the app may remove local data but does not necessarily remove data stored in iCloud. Manage iCloud storage and app access through Apple’s device settings.

This website

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, cookies, contact forms, or external fonts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact adaebea@gmail.com.
